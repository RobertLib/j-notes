# Privacy Policy — J-Notes

*Last updated: August 30, 2026*

## In short

J-Notes does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Your notes stay
on your device.

## In detail

**We collect no data.** The app never asks for your name, email address, phone
number or any other personal information. No account and no sign-in is required.

**It does not connect to the internet.** J-Notes contains no networking code and
makes no network requests. There is no cloud, no sync and no server behind the
app.

**No analytics, no advertising.** The app contains no ad networks, no analytics
frameworks and no third-party libraries that transmit anything anywhere.

**No in-app purchases.** The app contains no in-app purchases and no
subscriptions. Nothing in it is for sale.

**Where your notes are stored.** Notes — including their text, drawings,
background photos, tags, colours, reminder dates and locations — are stored
solely on your device, in a file inside the app's own container. That file is
written with complete file protection, which means iOS keeps it unreadable while
the device is locked. Your sort order, display style and the last position of the
map are stored in the system UserDefaults, and a short list of recent notes is
shared with the app's own widget through a private App Group container so the
widget can draw it. None of this data leaves the device. It is included in your
regular device backup (iCloud or computer) if you have one enabled, in which case
Apple's own policies apply. Deleting the app removes all of it from your device.

**Notes you lock.** A note you mark as protected is unlocked with Face ID, Touch
ID or your device passcode. Authentication is performed by iOS on the device; the
app never sees your biometric data, and there is no account or password held
anywhere.

**Location.** If you allow it, a note records the coordinates of the place where
it was created, so that the app can show your notes on a map. The location is
captured once, at creation, and is stored with the note on your device like any
other part of it. It is never transmitted. The app asks the system for a position
only when you do something that needs one — opening the map, pressing the locate
button, or creating a note — and refusing the permission only removes the map.

**Camera and photo library.** Used only when you choose a photo to draw on top
of. The image is stored inside the note on your device and is never uploaded.

**Notifications.** Reminders are local notifications scheduled by the app on your
device. Nothing is sent through a server. A locked note's reminder deliberately
reaches the Lock Screen without its title or its text.

**Sharing and backup are yours to start.** Sharing a note or a drawing hands it
to the system share sheet, and the backup file goes wherever you choose to save
it. Where the data goes next is decided by you and by the app or service you pick.

**Children.** The app is rated 4+. Because it collects no data, no data about
anyone of any age is created.
