A large update. Everything below is new since 1.0.9.

• Home Screen widgets in all three sizes and Lock Screen widgets in all three shapes — tapping a note in a widget opens that note, not the list
• A reminder shows as a banner even while the app is open, and tapping it opens the note it belongs to
• The badge counts only the reminders you have not seen yet, and pending reminders are re-numbered when one is set ahead of them
• A reminder iOS will not schedule is reported instead of being dropped silently, and the date you picked is kept
• Trashed notes can be opened before you decide, and the trash can be emptied in one go
• Tags that differ only in case are one tag, and a filter clears itself once no note carries it
• The map stays where you panned it, and the locate button works on the fix already on hand
• On iPad the list and the note sit side by side, through rotation and Split View alike
• Notes are written off the main thread, so ticking a checkbox no longer waits for the whole library
• VoiceOver reads a note's row and a checklist line as one element, and every icon-only control is labeled
• Text on the accent color now meets the 4.5:1 contrast ratio
• Dark and tinted app icon variants
